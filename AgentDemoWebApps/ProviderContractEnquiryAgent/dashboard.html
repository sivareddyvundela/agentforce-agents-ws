Provider Contract Inquiry

Overview

The Provider Contract Inquiry Agent automates contract lookups for contract-ops and network staff. It resolves a provider by contract number, name, or NPI, returns the effective/expiration dates and status, then drills into network tier, fee schedule, and amendment history on request — flagging anything expired or expiring within 30 days with a renewal recommendation.

What this agent automates

Instead of contract-ops staff manually searching spreadsheets or paging through PDFs, the agent answers natural-language questions directly:

  • Look up a contract by number, provider name, or NPI in a single turn.
  • Surface the healthcare provider name, contract number, and effective/expiration dates immediately.
  • Drill into provider network tier, fee schedule, or amendment history on follow-up request.
  • Automatically flag contracts that are expired or expiring within 30 days, with a renewal recommendation.
