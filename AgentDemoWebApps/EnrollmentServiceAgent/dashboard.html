Overview

The Enrollment Service Agent lets members request coverage changes — adding or removing a dependent — over chat or voice, without waiting on a phone queue. It collects the required details, opens a Case automatically, and hands back a 5-digit Issue Id for tracking. It can also escalate to a live representative on request.
